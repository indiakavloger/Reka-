01 / WHAT BROKE

More expensive reach exposed a weak buying story.

The old engine could grow while media was cheaper. As CPMs rose, a broad “quirky Gen Z” message stopped doing enough work between click and checkout.

Audience blur

“Gen Z” combines different jobs: gifting, self-expression, desk identity, dates, parties and smoking-corner utility.

FIX → build ad sets around moments, not a generation label.

Creative fatigue

The same product-led ad cannot sell a birthday gift and a “this is so me” impulse equally well.

FIX → rotate cultural hooks and product heroes weekly.

Conversion leak

At 1.2% CVR, scale compounds leakage. The ad promise, landing collection and price ladder must feel continuous.

FIX → send each angle to a matched collection, not the home page.

07 / 90-DAY SCALE ROADMAP

Earn the right to spend ₹10L every month.

The order is non-negotiable: clean measurement, prove audience × creative × landing fit, then scale. Festival weeks run on their own baseline so a temporary demand shift does not poison the account.

MONTH 1 · CONTROL

Map + test

  1. Launch six prospecting routes and three warm windows.
  2. Ship 6–8 concepts with three hooks per concept.
  3. Report by city, audience, product family and landing page.
  4. Hold ₹10L ceiling; do not force full spend into weak signals.
MONTH 2 · PROVE

Concentrate

  1. Move 70% of prospecting into top three route combinations.
  2. Graduate creator posts only after paid hook and CAC proof.
  3. Lift site CVR with message-matched collections and gifting proof.
  4. Build purchaser and high-AOV Lookalikes by source quality.
MONTH 3 · SCALE

Compound

  1. Increase winning ad sets by 15–20% every 48 hours.
  2. Duplicate only when a new city, audience or format needs isolation.
  3. Refresh hooks before frequency creates fatigue.
  4. Protect cash with a festival slowdown plan and post-festival re-entry.
GREEN → SCALECAC ≤ ₹600 + CVR ≥ 1.5%

Hold for three consecutive days with at least five purchases, then raise budget 15–20%. Recheck after 48 hours.

AMBER → FIXStrong CTR, weak checkout

Do not buy more clicks. Repair landing message, delivery proof, pricing ladder or mobile checkout before spend rises.

RED → CUT1.5× target CAC spent, no sale

Pause the ad, diagnose hook vs audience vs page, and recycle the learning—not the losing asset.

CVRby landing collection and device
CACby city, product family and first-touch angle

The pitch:

Joker Street should stop behaving like a quirky-product catalogue and start behaving like a culture brand with products built for specific moments.

Our first objective is not “10×.” It is to make the next rupee more predictable than the last—then scale what earns the right.

No growth or performance outcome is guaranteed. Results depend on creative quality, media costs, site conversion, margins, inventory, seasonality and execution.
